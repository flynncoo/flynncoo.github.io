AI use

In this section, I want to detail my use of AI in my research, and how that I ensure that my use of AI is responsible and transparent.

My views on AI evolved over time. At the start, when AI was a new tool, I was excited about the potential it offered as a kind of fancy autocomplete tool and to automate tasks that I considered dull like writing routine proofs of stupid lemmas and polishing exposition.

My opnions underwent a significant shift over Summer 2026, starting with the solution of the Jacobian conjecture, when I realised that AI was capable of automating tasks that I considered interesting and at the core of (my) intellectual life. I have adjusted my way of working in response: previously, I was chiefly motivated by trying to solve open problems, but now I regard that as a solved avenue of research and no longer attempt them. Instead I now work on developing new frameworks and theory with an eye towards applications.

I also do not use AI to produce text: for papers nor for teaching. On the other hand, I do use AI to produce code and numerical computations.
