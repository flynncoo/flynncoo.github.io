AI statement

I use AI tools for a) coding-related tasks primarily Codex and previously Claude code. b) as a search engine for the papers and other things in. the literature c) to review my work for mathematical correctness.

In the past, i.e., in 2025, I regarded writing as one of the lesser tasks of a researcher and I delegated some writing to Gemini, including proofs that I regarded as trivial or easily seen. Since the emergence of more powerful models in summer 2026, the community's views on this evolved and I now write all mathematical texts myself by hand.

I do not write papers where significant and novel ideas are contributed by AI as I regard this as immensely boring (though I imagine this may change at some point in the future). I am of the opinion that using AI to generate ideas is little different to taking them from other papers, a practice I refer to as "theorem-slop" which predates AI and should only be acceptable for PhD students but is common-place in our community.
